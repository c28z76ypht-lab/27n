Privacy policy

How 27n handles the data you share with us through this website.

Last updated: October 2026. Controller: 27n (Portugal). Contact: ola@27n.pt.

What we collect

When you submit a contact or careers form, we process the details you provide (name, email, company, phone, message and project interest) to reply and evaluate the request.

Why we process it

  • Respond to project and partnership enquiries
  • Schedule calls and share proposals
  • Handle spontaneous job applications
  • Improve the website experience when you accept analytics cookies

Cookies

We use essential cookies for theme preference and consent storage. Optional analytics cookies are only set if you choose “Accept all” on the cookie banner.

Sharing

Form submissions are processed via Web3Forms to deliver messages to our inbox. We do not sell personal data.

Retention & rights

We keep enquiry data only as long as needed to handle the conversation and related follow-up. You can request access, correction or deletion by emailing ola@27n.pt.
