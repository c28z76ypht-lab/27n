FAQ

Common ERP questions

Which ERPs do you integrate with Shopify?

PHC (Cegid), Sage, Cegid Primavera, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, Artsoft and Eticadata. SAP proceeds with a specialised SI partner.

Do you use middleware or only apps?

It depends: native APIs, apps and custom middleware with monitoring, logs, retries and alerts - so ops do not depend on fragile spreadsheet syncs.

Does this work with Shopify Plus and B2B?

Yes. On Plus we connect price lists, company accounts and multi-store setups to the ERP, plus checkout extensibility and Flow when useful.
