FAQ

Questions brands usually ask before starting.

How much does a Shopify project cost?

It depends on scope: custom theme, migrations, integrations and Plus features change the budget. After a discovery call we send a fixed proposal in 48–72h, no retainers required to get a clear number.

How long does a store take to launch?

A focused custom build typically lands in 6–10 weeks. Migrations with ERP integrations or multi-market setups can take longer. You get a roadmap with milestones from day one.

Can you migrate from WooCommerce, Magento or PrestaShop?

Yes. We migrate catalogue, customers, order history and SEO with 301 redirects URL by URL, so you don't lose organic traffic during the switch.

Do I need Shopify Plus?

Plus makes sense at higher volume or when you need checkout extensibility, B2B, advanced automation or expansion stores. We'll help you decide without upselling more than you need.

What happens after launch?

Launch is the start. Most clients stay with us for support, CRO and continuous improvement. We can also train your team to manage day-to-day catalog and content.

Do you only work with Shopify?

Yes. We've been a Shopify Select Partner since 2021 and focus exclusively on the platform: themes, Plus, Hydrogen, apps and growth.
