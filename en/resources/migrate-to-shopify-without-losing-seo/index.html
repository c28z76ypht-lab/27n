Migrate to Shopify without losing SEO: checklist

Checklist to migrate to Shopify without losing SEO: URL map, 301 redirects, metas, sitemap and Search Console.

Talk to 27n Want this applied to your project?

Rankings are not lost because you changed platforms. They are lost when old URLs become 404s. The plan:

  1. Export URLs with traffic (Search Console + sitemap + analytics).
  2. Map each old URL to the Shopify equivalent (/products/, /collections/, /pages/).
  3. Import 301 redirects before go-live.
  4. Carry over relevant titles and meta descriptions.
  5. Submit the new sitemap and monitor for 90 days.

Platform details: WooCommerce, Magento, PrestaShop.
