FAQ

Common questions

Does Medusa fit B2B?

Yes. Customer pricing, portals and orders between a company and its distributors sit in the same operation.

Where does the store website live?

On Astro or another frontend. Medusa handles commerce: products, cart, orders and stock.

When do you choose Medusa?

When the operation needs custom commerce, with its own pricing, stock and channel rules. 27n has been a Shopify Select Partner since 2021 for projects that stay on Shopify.
