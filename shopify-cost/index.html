Shopify plans

Prices Shopify publishes for Portugal.

Figures from the official table on 16 October 2026. The subscription is paid to Shopify. The 27n proposal covers the project: the store, migration, integrations and support.

Basic

€19/month billed yearly, or €27/month billed monthly. For solo businesses. Online cards from 1.8% + €0.30.

Grow

€56/month billed yearly, or €74/month billed monthly. Up to 5 staff accounts. Online cards from 1.6% + €0.30.

Advanced

€289/month billed yearly, or €384/month billed monthly. Up to 15 staff accounts, live third-party shipping rates and a store per region. Online cards from 1.5% + €0.30.

Shopify Plus

From €2,250/month on a 1-year term, or €2,100/month on a 3-year term. Larger operations can move to a variable platform fee. Online cards from 1.35% + €0.30, unlimited staff, a customizable checkout and unlimited B2B catalogs.

Shopify publishes a 3-day trial, then €1/month for 3 months. That offer can change. Extra Plus stores, after the main store and 9 expansion stores, cost €250/month each, or a revenue share.

Starter

From €5/month. For selling on social, with 1 POS login and a limited online store.

Retail

From €79/month. Unlimited POS logins and a limited online store. Includes phone and email in Shopify support.

POS Pro

€79/month per location on the published yearly price. Adds staff, inventory, customer profiles, retail analytics and online plus in-store selling on the same stock. Basic, Grow, Advanced and Plus already include occasional in-person selling.

Support

27n handles the project. Shopify handles the account.

Every plan

Chat in the Help Center, with search before a person joins. Shopify does not place outbound calls.

Plus, Retail and POS Pro

Plus and Retail add phone and email. POS Pro adds phone. Retail email typically replies within 48 hours.

Apps, themes and code

Third-party apps and paid themes stay with their makers. Custom code sits with 27n. Shopify supports its own free themes, with a block of design time from Basic upward.
